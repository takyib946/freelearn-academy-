ICT

Introduction to Computing

Learn about computers, hardware, software, and data.

What is a computer?

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it, stores it, and produces information.

Basic computer components

  • Input: Keyboard and mouse.
  • Processing: The CPU processes instructions.
  • Output: Monitor and printer.
  • Storage: SSD, hard drive, and flash drive.

Remember: Data is raw facts; information is processed data that has meaning.

English

English Grammar Basics

Improve your sentences, vocabulary, and communication.

Parts of speech

  • Noun: A person, place, thing, or idea.
  • Verb: An action or state, such as learn or be.
  • Adjective: Describes a noun, such as good.
  • Adverb: Describes an action, such as slowly.

Example sentence

"I am learning English every day."

"I" is the subject; "am learning" is the verb phrase.

Cybersecurity

Online Safety Basics

Understand passwords, phishing, and safer internet use.

Protect your accounts

  • Use unique, strong passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Never share verification codes.
  • Check links before opening them.

What is phishing?

Phishing is a trick used to persuade people to reveal passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Practise security testing only on systems you own or have clear permission to test.
